Key Insights

Analysis of sales data revealed important patterns and opportunities:

  • Sales Trends: Sales peaked in March, with a steady decline in both sales value and order count from March onwards through December. This may indicate strong seasonality or a campaign effect early in the year.
  • Top Sales Channels: Amazon holds the largest sales share among channels (35.48%), followed by Flipkart (21.59%) and Myntra (23.36%), highlighting where most customers prefer to place their orders.
  • Order Status: An overwhelming majority (92%) of orders are delivered, with very low percentages for returned (3%), refunded (2%), and cancelled (3%) orders, reflecting efficient fulfillment and customer satisfaction.
  • Gender-wise Sales: Women make up 64% of the sales, while men contribute to 36%, indicating a customer base that is predominantly female.
  • Customer Segmentation: The age and gender breakdown shows adult women are the largest segment (34.59%), followed by teenage girls (21.13%). Male adults and teenagers represent smaller shares, suggesting targeted marketing opportunities.
  • Geographic Performance: The top 5 performing states are Maharashtra (2.99M sales), Karnataka (2.65M), Uttar Pradesh (2.10M), Telangana (1.71M), and Tamil Nadu (1.68M), showing strong demand in both West, South, and North India.

Conclusion

The dashboard reveals that while Amazon leads as a channel and female customers, especially adults and teenagers, are the primary audience, there is a noticeable seasonal peak in early months. The company’s delivery efficiency is high, with most orders successfully fulfilled and few returns or cancellations. Focusing marketing efforts on women in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and other top states, particularly during peak months, could further boost performance and sales growth.
